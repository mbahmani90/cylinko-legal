3. Permissions the app asks for and why

  • Location (Android): required by the OS to scan for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi devices. We use it only to discover devices nearby; we do not record or transmit your location.
  • Bluetooth: to discover and pair devices over BLE.
  • Wi-Fi / local network / multicast: to find and talk to devices on your local network.
  • Microphone: only when you tap the microphone button, to convert speech to text for the AI assistant. Audio is processed by the platform's speech service and is not stored by us.
  • Storage / files: to import or export files you choose.
  • Camera (if enabled): to scan QR codes for pairing or sharing.

You can revoke any permission in your OS settings; the related feature will stop working.

4. Third parties we use

  • Amazon Web Services (Cognito, AppSync, DynamoDB, IoT Core, Lambda, S3) — authentication, data storage, device messaging. Data is stored in the AWS region [region].
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase — payments. We never see your card details.
  • Google Gemini / OpenAI — only if you add your own API key. Your prompts, and transcribed speech, are sent directly to the provider you chose, under that provider's privacy policy. Your key is stored only on your device.

We do not sell your data.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account from Profile → Delete account in the app. This removes your account, devices, channels, templates and sharing records, and forfeits any remaining CyCoin. Billing records may be kept as described above where the law requires. You can also email [support email].

7. Children
